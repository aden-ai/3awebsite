What's inside

Modules and their features

01

Finance & Accounting

Tracks accounts payable and receivable and manages the general ledger. Automates billing, vendor payments, cash management and reconciliation, and produces P&L statements and board reports.

02

Procurement

Maintains approved vendor lists, automates requests for quotes, and turns accepted quotes into tracked purchase orders that update inventory automatically on arrival.

03

Manufacturing (Production)

Plans production runs, tracks goods-in-progress against forecast, and gives a real-time picture of the shop floor — from raw materials to finished goods.

04

Inventory Management

Tracks item quantities and location down to SKU level, weighs sales trends against stock on hand, and helps prevent stockouts across multiple locations.

05

Order Management

Tracks every order from receipt to delivery, feeding warehouse and fulfilment teams while boosting on-time delivery rates.

06

Warehouse Management

Guides staff through putaway, picking and packing based on facility layout, and supports batch, wave and zone picking strategies.

07

Supply Chain Management

Tracks every step in the movement of goods from supplier to consumer, and manages returns for refund or replacement.

08

Customer Relationship Management

Stores customer and prospect data, communication history and purchase history, and supports lead management and segmentation.

09

Professional Services Automation

Plans and manages projects, tracks human and capital resources, approves expenses and timesheets, and automates client billing.

10

Workforce Management

Monitors attendance and hours, measures productivity, and handles payroll disbursal with tax deductions and reimbursements.

11

Human Resources Management

Holds detailed employee records — performance reviews, job descriptions, offer letters, PTO and benefits — in one place.

12

Marketing Automation

Runs campaigns across email, web, social and SMS with rule-based automation and customer segmentation.

Common questions

Frequently asked questions

What is ERP?
ERP stands for Enterprise Resource Planning — an integrated software solution used to manage a company's resources. It brings planning, projects, inventory, engineering, order processing, manufacturing, purchasing, accounting, finance, HR and payroll into one system.
Why choose ERP Power Pro over another ERP?
It's built specifically for manufacturing and trading operations, with accounting, payroll and HR native to the same system — so financial data never needs re-entry elsewhere, and every department works from one source of truth.
How long does implementation take?
3A Solutions packages two decades of deployment experience into a set of proven, stepped implementations, so go-live happens in a predictable timeframe rather than an open-ended rollout.
Is the system scalable for growing teams?
Yes. ERP Power Pro can scale resources up or down to meet current needs, and supports deployment models — from on-premise to hybrid — that grow with the business.
Do we need a dedicated IT person to run it?
Not necessarily — cloud and hybrid deployment options shift maintenance, updates and security to 3A Solutions, reducing the IT burden on your team.
What hardware and software do we need?
ERP Power Pro runs on SQL Server 2012 or 2019 with a .NET Framework 4.0 client. Talk to us about your current environment and we'll confirm exact requirements for your team size.
