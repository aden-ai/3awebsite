Data security & confidentiality

We treat your data like it's ours to lose.

3A Solutions takes data security and confidentiality seriously — with systems and measures built to protect against breaches and unauthorized access, and to preserve the trust our clients place in us.

NDA on every engagement. Every employee signs one internally, and we sign one with you before work begins.

Access restricted by floor and role. Only employees with permission reach the specific client data their assignment requires.

Locked-down endpoints. Complex password policy, USB drives disabled, and C:/ D:/ drive access blocked.

AWS-hosted, IDS/IPS protected. Gateway-level firewalls and intrusion detection on every environment we run.

Annual VAPT scans. We test ourselves every year — and clients are welcome to run their own audit on us.
